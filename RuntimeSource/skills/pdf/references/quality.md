# PDF generation quality

Decide the document archetype first: memo, report, SOP, form, proposal, manual, certificate, worksheet, poster, or visual brief. Choose typography, spacing, heading hierarchy, margins, table style, and visual emphasis before generating pages.

Use readable body sizes and line spacing. Prefer hierarchy, spacing, alignment, and consistent typography over decorative styling. Avoid dense walls of text when structure or a visual anchor improves comprehension.

For tables, choose widths based on content rather than equal widths. Keep short values compact, narrative fields wider, let rows grow with wrapping, and provide enough padding so text never touches borders or clips. Repeat headers on multi-page tables where possible.

Design forms for completion rather than as spreadsheets. Give check targets and fields enough space and keep labels clearly associated with inputs.

Use high-resolution source images and readable chart labels. Keep captions paired with figures and never stretch raster images disproportionately.

Avoid large blank gaps from pushed tables/figures and stranded headings. Render at about 180-220 DPI and inspect every page for clipping, overlap, missing glyphs, unexpected blanks, bad page breaks, crowded cells, raster blur, or text too close to borders.
